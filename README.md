# The Architect

## Character Introduction

Play as the **Architect**, a new character for *Slay the Spire 2*. Face the beings you created and climb toward a mystery: if two Architects await at the top of the Spire, who is the true god?

The mod adds 90 character cards, 9 unique Relics, 3 unique Potions, and a unique Curse, **Slumber**, alongside custom character art, card illustrations, and UI assets.

The Architect's gameplay revolves around **Enchantments**. Enchant cards during combat, trigger powerful synergies, remove Enchantments for immediate rewards, or refresh them to activate their effects again. Replaying key cards helps you build momentum throughout a fight.

Some cards generate Slumber as a cost. Any Slumber left unexhausted at the end of combat enters your master deck, while playing it removes the corresponding Curse. Balance power now against the burden of future battles.
