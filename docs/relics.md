# 建筑师遗物说明 / Architect Relic Descriptions

| 中文名称 | English Name | 中文描述 | English Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 空白圣典 | Blank Codex | 回合结束时，若本回合打出过至少1张牌，且没有打出附魔牌，获得{Block}点格挡。 | At the end of your turn, if you played at least 1 card and no Enchanted cards this turn, gain {Block} Block. |
| 校准尺 | Calibration Ruler | 每回合首次成功为一张未附魔的牌附魔时，获得{Block}点格挡。 | The first time each turn you successfully Enchant an unenchanted card, gain {Block} Block. |
| 誊写羽笔 | Copyist’s Quill | 每回合首次打出一张本场战斗已经打出过的牌后，抽1张牌。 | The first time each turn you play a card you have already played this combat, draw 1 card. |
| 拆解钳 | Dismantling Pliers | 每回合首次主动褪去一张牌的附魔时，获得1点能量。转移附魔不计入。 | The first time each turn you actively remove Enchantments from a card, gain 1 Energy. Transferring Enchantments does not count. |
| 定稿印章 | Finalizing Seal | 获得时，选择主牌堆中的1张牌。每场战斗中，每当打出这张牌后，刷新其仍保有的附魔。不恢复已褪去的附魔。 | Upon pickup, choose a card in your deck. Whenever you play that card in combat, refresh its remaining Enchantments afterward. Does not restore removed Enchantments. |
| 奠基罗盘 | Foundational Compass | 每场战斗开始时，从你起始手牌中随机选择1张可附魔的牌，赋予其1种随机基础附魔（灵巧2、锋利2、播种1、迅速2或本能2）。 | At the start of each combat, randomly choose 1 card in your starting hand that can be Enchanted. Give it a random basic Enchantment (Nimble 2, Sharp 2, Sown 1, Swift 2, or Instinct 2). |
| 复写羊皮卷 | Palimpsest | 每场战斗首次将同一张牌打出至第3次后，获得2点能量，抽2张牌。 | Once per combat, after you play the same card for the third time, gain 2 Energy and draw 2 cards. |
| 回流墨瓶 | Reflow Inkwell | 回合结束时，若本回合打出过至少3张不同的附魔牌，将弃牌堆中1张随机附魔牌置于抽牌堆顶。 | At the end of your turn, if you played at least 3 different Enchanted cards, put a random Enchanted card from your discard pile on top of your draw pile. |
